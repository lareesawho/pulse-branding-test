(Featured transformation — WeKwikGene)

Complex science. Clear experience.

We helped turn a nascent scientific platform into an identity and digital experience built to travel across China’s research community.

(Context)

A platform built for scientists had to feel scientifically credible — without feeling difficult.

WeKwikGene is a plasmid distribution platform connected to Westlake Laboratory. The work had to support real scientific functionality while making the experience accessible enough to spread across a wider research community.

01

The challenge

Scientific complexity, multiple stakeholder needs and a platform that needed identity, trust and usable digital infrastructure at the same time.

02

The insight

The platform could not behave like a generic repository. The scientific precision itself had to become part of the brand experience.

03

The direction

Make the complexity feel controlled: clear navigation, disciplined visual logic and a brand system credible enough for researchers.

04

The build

Brand, UX/UI and product implementation shaped as one connected system rather than separate deliverables.

(Outcome)

From nascent idea to a robust scientific platform.

The platform expanded quickly through China’s scientific community and became a high-visibility project within its institutional ecosystem. The strongest evidence is the client’s own description of the work: scientific precision, accessibility and flexibility all mattered to launch.

“Pulse Branding introduced creative features and visuals with scientific precision, enhancing our vision and broadening our perspectives.”

Dr. Kiryl D. Piatkevich · Neurobiology Professor · Westlake Laboratory
